# GTM principles

The rules behind every campaign we run.

## 1. Small lists beat broad lists

A tight, specific list with a reason to reach out beats a big generic one.

## 2. Fundamentals beat fancy tactics

Right person, right reason, clear offer. Tricks come after.

## 3. Value before the ask

Give something useful first. The meeting comes from the reply.

## 4. One message, one idea

Every email has one job. Earn the reply, not the meeting, in step 1.

## 5. No copy saves a bad offer

If the offer is weak, rewrite the offer, not the subject line.

## 6. The goal is a reply

Nothing more. A short, easy question beats a calendar link.
